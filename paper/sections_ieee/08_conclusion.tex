\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A setup before an objective is an allocation of five players on a walled map against a public clock.
Measured that way, professional teams behave consistently: trailing teams leave the pit empty after the spawn, setups follow a small number of reproducible walks on the partition lattice, and commitment responds to fight odds, travel and the cost of each player far more than to the prize itself.
None of this requires a neural embedding, and much of what an embedding pipeline would report changes with the seed or the estimator.

The same objects apply wherever teams split players between a contested point and the rest of the map: other MOBAs, tactical shooters and team sports with set pieces.
The tracks, setups and code are released with the paper.
