\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Positions come from the broadcast minimap; with four or more other champions nearby only \V{trkCrowdFourLtSix} of positions are within 600 units, and formations agree exactly with Riot's in \V{formExact} of snapshots, which is why every headline rests on sign agreement across radii, an R-layer check or a sensitivity run without interpolated positions.
Vision, cooldowns, wave states and voice communication are unobserved, so every behavioural statement is conditional on the observed state.
The data cover one rule set and two international events; teams with few games carry wide intervals.
LB-QRE reproduces about a quarter of the observed commitment gap, almost all of it through state variables rather than its strategic channels; the rest is behaviour the allocation game in its present form does not capture.
Win probability and event values are outcome-only associations from the archive, not causal effects.
